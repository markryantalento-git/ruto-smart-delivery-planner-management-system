Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RUTO handles personal information for its transportation and delivery planning service. It is intended to follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable National Privacy Commission guidance, including NPC Circular No. 2023-04.

Effective when published. RUTO is currently free to use; there are no subscriptions, trials, or payment walls.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Mark Ryan Talento is the personal information controller for RUTO. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact ruto.supportph@gmail.com. Include the email address associated with your account and describe your request. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account; do not email your password or verification codes.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, phone number and sign-in provider where used, from Firebase Authentication and information you provide.
  • Profile photo: a photo you choose and crop. The image is stored in Firebase Storage; its download URL may be saved in your account record and Firebase Authentication profile.
  • Consent and account record: whether you accepted this policy version and the time Firebase recorded that consent, along with account creation and app-version information.
  • Delivery and route information: pickup and saved GPS coordinates, place names, delivery stops, schedules, cargo details, vehicle choices, route preferences, and estimated costs saved in Cloud Firestore when signed in. Some information may also be kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Location: when you grant browser permission, the app may use the device's current coordinates to center nearby-place searches, set an origin, or support live navigation while the app is open. Location permission can be denied or revoked in your device or browser settings.
  • Preferences and technical data: settings such as theme and language, session state, whether you selected “Keep me logged in on this device,” and locally cached app data needed for the service to work. In a regular browser, selecting this option keeps your Firebase sign-in on this device until you sign out or clear its site data; leaving it unselected limits sign-in to the current browser session. Installed standalone apps retain sign-in so they can open directly into RUTO until you sign out or clear site data. RUTO does not intentionally collect precise location in the background.

3. Why we use the information

We use information to create and secure accounts, save and show your plans, calculate route and vehicle estimates, display nearby places, provide navigation and requested assistant features, remember preferences, respond to support requests, and maintain the service. We use GPS only after your browser asks and you grant permission. We do not sell personal information or use your private route history for advertising.

Account and route data are processed to provide the features you request, administer your account, and meet legal obligations. Before account creation, RUTO presents a separate, unchecked consent box describing the account and GPS route data involved; consent is recorded only after you actively check it. This is intended to support the freely given, specific, informed, and evidenced consent principles in NPC Circular No. 2023-04, Guidelines on Consent. Where consent is the applicable basis, you may withdraw it as described below. Withdrawing consent may prevent use of account-based features.

4. Service providers and sharing

RUTO uses Google Firebase for Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, and (when enabled) Firebase AI Logic. Google and its service providers process information to provide these services under their terms and privacy commitments. Firebase data may be processed or stored outside the Philippines, subject to the provider's safeguards and applicable law.

If you choose Facebook sign-in, Meta processes the information needed to authenticate you under its own privacy terms. RUTO receives the authentication details Firebase makes available for your account.

Some features also send the minimum information needed to external providers: OpenStreetMap services and Overpass/Photon for map tiles and nearby-place or address searches; OSRM or an optional configured TomTom service for routes; Open-Meteo for requested weather; and Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic when the AI assistant is enabled. A map or route request can include coordinates. AI prompts are sent only when you use the assistant. The assistant does not intentionally save chat history to your RUTO account.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to a successor operator subject to appropriate privacy safeguards. We do not otherwise share account or route data with advertisers.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Signed-in account and delivery records are stored in Firebase services. The browser may also retain session, preferences, and locally saved planning data. In a regular browser, selecting “Keep me logged in on this device” uses persistent Firebase Authentication storage until you sign out or clear site data; leaving it unselected uses session-only sign-in. Installed standalone apps retain authentication for direct app startup regardless of this browser preference. We use access controls and Firebase Security Rules intended to limit cloud access to the account owner. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

We keep account and route records while needed to provide RUTO and until you request deletion, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve a dispute. Local copies remain on your device until you clear the browser's site data. External providers have their own retention practices.

6. Your rights under RA 10173

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in law, you may exercise the following rights:

  • Access: request confirmation and a copy or description of personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information. You can edit profile details and saved plans in the app where those controls are available.
  • Erasure or blocking / account deletion: request deletion of your RUTO account and associated cloud profile, saved deliveries, notifications, and profile photo, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully performed and may mean that account-based or location-dependent features can no longer be provided.
  • Other rights: you may have rights to object, data portability, damages, and to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission, as provided by law.

To make a request, email ruto.supportph@gmail.com with the subject “RUTO privacy request,” state the right you wish to exercise, and provide the account email. For account deletion, explicitly say “delete my RUTO account.” We will verify the request, respond within the period required by applicable law, and explain any lawful reason we cannot fully comply. Deleting your Firebase Authentication account alone may not automatically erase Firestore subcollections or browser copies; ask for full RUTO data deletion and clear RUTO site data on your devices after confirmation.

7. Children and policy updates

RUTO is a general transportation planning tool and is not directed to children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date; when required, we will request fresh consent.

8. Contact and complaints

Contact ruto.supportph@gmail.com for privacy inquiries, correction, access, consent withdrawal, or deletion requests. You may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission through its official channels if you believe your rights have been violated.
